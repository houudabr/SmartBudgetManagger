# SmartBudgetManagger
A project for managing Budget.
What it does
SmartBudget Manager records your monthly income and expenses, sorts expenses by category, calculates your balance automatically, shows a chart on the Dashboard and warns you when you exceed your budget limit.
What you need
•	Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365 (or a compatible spreadsheet program).
•	Windows or macOS computer. No internet connection and no macros required.
Contents
Worksheet	Purpose
Dashboard	Monthly balance, total income, total expenses, charts, budget warning
Transactions	Where you enter income and expenses
Categories	Predefined list: Food, Transport, Rent, Health, Education
Quick start
1.	Double-click SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx. If a yellow bar appears, click Enable Editing.
2.	Open the Transactions worksheet (tab at the bottom).
3.	Enter each transaction in the first empty row: Date, Description, Category, then Income or Expense.
4.	On the Dashboard, type your Monthly Budget Limit.
5.	Open the Dashboard to read your balance and charts.
6.	Press Ctrl+S to save.
Good to know
•	Only type in the white cells; grey/locked cells contain formulas.
•	A red total means you have exceeded your budget
